How it works

Ten seconds a day. That’s it.

  1. 1

    Pick four tiny habits

    Small enough to do on your worst day: a glass of water, five pages, one stretch.

  2. 2

    Tick them off

    From the app or straight from your home screen. No timers, no journaling, no guilt.

  3. 3

    Watch it grow

    Every habit adds a leaf. Keep a streak and it flowers. Miss a day and it naps, then perks back up.

Features

Small app. Thoughtful details.

  • Home-screen widget

    Your plant lives on your home screen, so ticking a habit never means opening an app.

  • Gentle reminders

    One nudge at the time you choose. Never a red badge, never a guilt trip.

  • Streak freeze

    Sick, travelling, or just tired? Freeze your streak for a day and your plant stays put.

  • Shared gardens

    Plant side by side with up to eight friends. You see who showed up, not what they did.

  • Private by default

    No account needed. Your habits stay on your phone unless you choose to share a garden.

  • Works offline

    Tick habits on the train, in the woods, in airplane mode. It syncs when you’re back.

FAQ

Good questions.

Why only four habits?

Because four is the number people actually keep. You can swap any of them whenever you like.

What happens if I miss a day?

Your plant naps. It droops a little, then perks back up the next day you show up. Nothing ever dies.

Do I need an account?

No. Everything stays on your phone unless you turn on shared gardens.

Can I name my plant?

You have to. It’s the first thing the app asks. Kevin is a popular choice.
